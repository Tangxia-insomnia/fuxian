\textbf{Labeling process.} For each warning, two participants will individually label it as True or False; for the inconsistent cases, a third judge will be introduced to solve the conflicts. If the participants cannot reach agreement after discussion, the warning would be filtered out. All the participants have over five-year experience on C/C++; and each would received 200 dollars for the labeling. The experiments are approved by Ethics Review Board.
